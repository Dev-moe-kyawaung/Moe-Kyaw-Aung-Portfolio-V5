FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What technologies do you specialize in for Android development?

I specialize in Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Android Architecture Components (ViewModel, LiveData, Navigation), Room, DataStore, Hilt/Dagger for DI, Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics), Retrofit for REST APIs, and Coroutines/Flow for async programming.

Do you offer remote freelance services internationally?

Yes! I work fully remote with clients worldwide. I communicate in both English and Myanmar, and I'm comfortable with time zones across Asia, Europe, and the Americas. I use GitHub, Jira, Slack, and Azure DevOps for project collaboration.

What is your development process and timeline?

I follow an Agile/Scrum methodology with 1–2 week sprints. After an initial discovery call, I provide a detailed technical specification and timeline. I set up a CI/CD pipeline from day one and provide regular build previews. Final delivery includes full documentation, source code, and deployment support.

Can you work with existing codebases or only greenfield projects?

Both! I'm experienced in legacy code migration — modernizing Java/XML Android apps to Kotlin/Jetpack Compose, introducing Clean Architecture to existing projects, setting up testing (JUnit, Espresso, MockK), and establishing CI/CD for mature codebases. I'll assess the existing code and provide an honest improvement roadmap.

What certifications do you hold and how relevant are they?

I hold 49+ professional certifications from Programming Hub (in partnership with Google Developers Launchpad) and other providers. These cover Android, Kotlin, Firebase, React, Python, AI/ML, Cyber Security, AWS, Azure, DevOps, Git, and many more. While certifications complement practical experience, my portfolio of production apps is the best demonstration of my skills.

Do you provide post-launch support and maintenance?

Yes. Enterprise packages include ongoing maintenance. For other packages, I offer a 30-day post-launch support period for bug fixes. Long-term maintenance retainer agreements are available for clients who need continuous improvements, dependency updates, and feature additions.
